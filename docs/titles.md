## Reading an entry

Each section says what the title did in macrt. Command-line options
such as `--model ii`, `--host screen` or `--memory 4096` are
[macrt's own](running-apps.md#running-it), and `--app` names the
program on a disk image by its Mac path. Where a section gives
`--wait`, `--steps` or a step such as `c 320,240` (a click at that
point on the screen), those are the [survey tool's](survey.md),
which ran the title headless.

When you try another, the list of missing calls it prints when it quits
(see [When it stops](running-apps.md#when-it-stops)) is what to report.

## Numbers and symbols

### 1987 HyperCard Supplement, DM 3000

HyperCard stacks, which open with HyperCard 2.4's boot disk after their
own: `annual_report.img HyperCardBootSystem7.img --app "1987 HyperCard
Supplement" --model ii --depth 8 --size 640x480 --host screen`. Apple's
supplement to its 1987 annual report opens on Welcome to the HyperCard
Supplement, and a click goes on. DM 3000, from the disk beside it
(`disk.img`, `--app "DM 3000"`), opens on Death Mall 3000's title and
story, with RECAP, QUIT and MOVE ON.

### 2nd Terror Protocol, Emergency Blow!, Emergency Blow! v.2, Space Can Suck!

Run, with `--model ii --depth 8 --size 640x480 --host screen`, from the
disk of Mobuli's games (`--app "2TP:2nd Terror Protocol"`, `--app
"Emergency Blow! v1.0"`, `--app "Emergency Blow!v.2 :Emergency
Blow!v.2"` and `--app "Space Can Suck! v.2"`). Each opens on its notes
beside a picture; the last three's Quit, Open Game and Begin Game are
under them.

### 3 in Three

Plays its story, with `--app "3 in Three™" --host screen --size
512x342`: click to continue (in its menu bar) from one cartoon to the
next.

### 5 Stix, Dr. Macinto, Polytris, PolyMine, Tangram, Poly-Peg

Play, in colour, with `--model ii --depth 8 --size 640x480 --host
screen`, from the disk of S.T. Han's games (`--app "Polytris:Polytris
1.09"` and so on). Demo goes past 5 Stix's and Tangram's registration
dialogs to their boards; OK past Polytris's title starts its pieces
falling, and past PolyMine's deals its board. Dr. Macinto and Poly-Peg
open on their boards.

### The 500 Mac Games CD

`500MacGames.iso`, 472 shareware and freeware games, demos and game
editors in folders by kind, has no System Folder: give it BMUG's with
`--system BMUGPDROMVolume1.img` and name each program by its path, as
`--app "Cards:Spoydworks Solitaire 1.2 ƒ:Spoydworks Solitaire 1.2"`,
with `--host screen`.

On the default Plus all but two run without stopping with an error; Orb
Mazez and MacCribbage (Color) stop there, and run with `--model ii
--depth 8 --size 640x480`, Orb Mazez to its title and MacCribbage to its
choice of who deals first. Rescue! makes its offscreen world with
`NewGWorld` on a Plus; Poly-Dol passes `UseResFile` a refnum, 2, that's
no resource file's (it's the boot volume's extents tree), so it goes on
finding its own resources; BrickBounce and Spoydworks Solitaire won't
start without the Edition Manager, and Spoydworks, on a Mac II, finds
its Games & Decks folder in a dialog that picks a folder (see [What
works](running-apps.md#what-works)), then deals its eleven games with
any of its sixteen decks; CyberBlast and the Deliverance demo call
`RsrcMapEntry` at startup, and CyberBlast plays; and the CyberFlix
previews (Jump Raven, Lunicus, Western) read the video card's
declaration ROM to see which depths the screen has.

On a Mac II in colour, `--model ii --depth 8 --size 640x480`, all but
one run without an error: Race Car, a MacFORTH program, has its own
handler for `TRAP #15` that takes the exception frame to be the 68000's
six bytes, and the 68020 pushes eight, so it runs only on the Plus.
MacTriv passes `RectRgn` the address of its rectangle for the region,
which isn't a handle in any heap, and macrt leaves it alone.

Some want more than a launch: MS BASIC Runtime runs its ADVENTURE
program when that's named as the document (`--app "Adventure:ADVENTURE
ƒ:ADVENTURE"`), Blocker plays after File › Open Game Map and File › Play
the Game, Macinsplat's clicks draw once a weapon is picked from Options,
and Ninja Boy, RoadKill and Populous ask for 16 colours. Vanlandingham
wants `--size 512x342`: it sizes a one-bit buffer from the screen, and
on 640×480 that's 38,400 bytes, past what a 16-bit integer holds, so it
asks `NewPtr` for a negative size and says there isn't enough memory.
CrossWise needs `--memory 320`, as on BMUG's Volume 2. Snow draws
straight to the screen as if each row were 64 bytes, a 512-pixel
screen's, so it too wants `--size 512x342`. The Falcon MC demo wants
`--depth 4`: it looks through the ADB Manager's device table for a
device of its own and, finding none, opens on its Duty Roster. On a Mac
II at 8 bits, Pathways into Darkness's Command-N asks in a Save dialog
where to keep a new player, and once one is named it draws its view of
the pyramid, which the arrow keys walk through (in a survey, `C n; k
Player\n`).

On a Mac II at 8 bits, some open no window until asked: Command-N gives
Moria 1.0b2's character creation, deals Poker Solitaire v0.9ß, opens
SpaceBattle 1.01's Game Window, where the battle runs, and has Bombs 2.1
ask the board's size, Progression v1.5 a player's name and Karma Manager
a starting word or phrase; MacHeads › Play Game opens MacHeads' board,
and PowerBall's Manage › Lottery Numbers its grid of numbers. Megaroids
][ says the Macintosh must be in two colour mode to start a game, and
with `--depth 1` File › Start Game plays. 'bot Developer 1.0.1 asks at
once, in an Open dialog, for a `.bot` file, and Ars Magna's console for
its dictionary files: after two Returns, a phrase and two more (to print
to the screen, a page at a time) it lists the phrase's anagrams. Tetris
2000 plays from Game › New Game, but its Command keys do nothing: its
key handler hands the typed character to its menu routine in place of
the menu and item, without calling MenuKey. Robot Warriors 1.0.1 opens
only hidden windows at launch; given a robot (`--app "Action:Robot
Warriors 1.0.1 ƒ:Example Robots:Seeker"`) it opens the robot's program,
Battle › Compile Robot compiles it, and once a second (Chicken, from
File › Open…) is compiled too, Battle › Start Battle… lists both and the
battle runs to its Game Over and statistics. Castles Demo shows its
MacPlay title; while `TickCount` was under 180, as it was when macrt's
tick clock started at 0, its idle loop ran past the end of a list of its
own and over its own records, and it quit. DeadEnd 1.0 checks sums of
its own dialogs and pictures, which its routine leaves in D0 after
`ReleaseResource`, and counts the sound channels it can have by asking
for one more until `SndNewChannel` refuses; past its title (a click) and
a Start at Level dialog (Return), level 1 plays with the arrow keys.
Distant Early Warning opens the `.XPP` driver first and quits when it
can't: macrt has no AppleTalk drivers. Change Type to Moria Save quits
once it has handled the Open Application event macrt sends it at launch,
with no documents. Tron is for two players, one on W A S D and the other
on I J K L; it quits on a collision or a mouse press, and left alone the
two cycles meet head-on and it quits within about seven seconds.

On a Mac II at 8 bits most of the game editors, updaters and installers
open no window until asked, and their Open commands (and the like) ask
for a file in a dialog that starts in the program's own folder:
GameMaster's lists its games, and BattleShips opens with its two boards;
PoPMap looks for `LEVL` resources in the file it's given, and given
itself says it has none; and LockSmith's Make Alias…, given AutoLock™
Demo, says it may not make an AutoLock document for AutoLock™. In
Gauntlet Editor, SC_Edit, DeckEdit, DM Updater, DM Ambient Sound
Installer and Pathways Into Cheating the dialog lists nothing in that
folder, and Pathways Into Cheating's New… is dimmed. SimCity Editor's
New City… opens its Variable dialog at External Market Size. Deep
Thought Viewer asks at launch for the file to view, and shows Deep
Thought 2.0.3's 4,231 records, and Update TM 2.0->2.0.1 and the two
Shanghai updaters ask at launch for the copy to update, in dialogs of
their own, whose text macrt shows under the list.

### ][ in a Mac

### ][ in a Mac

Runs, with `--host screen --size 512x342`, from its MOOF image. It's an
Apple II emulator, which draws the Apple's screen straight into the
Mac's screen memory, and it boots the Apple DOS 3.3 disk that comes with
it to its menu. Press 2 for Applesoft BASIC: type a program and `RUN`
it, `CATALOG` lists the disk and `HGR` draws in high resolution. `RUN
LITTLE BRICK OUT` plays: Options › Use Joystick makes the mouse the
paddle and a click its button, and Command-click gives the mouse back.

### A CD of game demos

`MacDemoGames.img`, with System 7.5.5 and fifty demos in its More
Information folder, each named by its path, as `--app "More
Information:Tristan™:TRISTAN™ DEMO"`, with `--model ii --depth 8 --size
640x480 --host screen`. Nearly all reach their titles or play: Prince of
Persia's palace, Battle Chess's board, Color Super Tetris, Crystal
Quest, Lemmings, Glider 4.06, Spectre, Xanthus, SimAnt and Warlords past
their notices, and Tesserae's mosaic. Mission Starlight turns its sound
on by setting a bit of the VIA's with `BitSet`, and goes on to its game
screen. Tristan opens its External Sound file again for each game and
looks for its sounds there, and plays. Pararena asks for 16 or 2 colours
and, given 16, sets the screen to them itself and shows its title. OIDS,
RoboSport, Color VETTE! and Armor Alley want 2 or 16 colours, and run
with `--depth 4`; Armor Alley also runs at 256 colours with `--memory
5000`. Mission Thunderbolt needs `--memory 6000`, and without it says
its memory is critically low. Checkmate is a black-and-white demo and
says so. The Journeyman Project's Sneak Preview and The Iron Helix's
demo play their music from AIFF files with play from disk: Journeyman
goes on past its burned city into the time machine, and Iron Helix shows
its walkthrough on the ship's console, then its title, and quits when it
is over, as it should. Rommel and Panzer Battles want 16 colours, or 2
for their black-and-white scenarios, and run with `--depth 4` or
`--depth 1`; each asks for a scenario in an Open dialog and goes on to
its Game Preparation dialog. Rodney's Funscreen shows its tent, and a
click on a game, such as Guess-O-Matic, starts it.

### Apple's Productivity drive

`Productivity.img`, a hard disk with a System 7 System Folder whose
Fonts folder has outline fonts, runs its programs without `--system`,
each named by its path, as `--app "MacDraw Pro Folder:MacDraw Pro"`. All
22 programs tried run on the default Plus, and all but one on a Mac II
(`--model ii --depth 8 --size 640x480`): the two ClarisWorks in Claris
Files open the New Document dialog, and the one in ClarisWorks asks to
be personalized; BBEdit Lite 3.0 and TextChanger 1.0.1 open a file
dialog; Kid Pix asks for your name; MacDraw Pro opens a drawing with its
palettes; MacWrite 1.0, 2.2 and 5.0 an Untitled document with its ruler;
Photoshop 0.63b its tools; HyperCard 2.4 its Home stack; TeachText,
SimpleText and the Scriptable Text Editor an untitled document. The
DOCMaker issues of About This Particular Macintosh, ATPM 1.01 to 5.07,
open: the later ones ask after sizes of Geneva and Helvetica that the
disk has as outline fonts, which `RealFont` says are there at any size.
The Italian MacWrite 4.5 in Articles&stuff runs on the Plus; on a Mac II
it stops with system error 3, as its own `TRAP #4` handler takes a
